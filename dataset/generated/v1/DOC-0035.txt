# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

## 1. Parties and Scope
This Agreement is made between Northwind Industries (the Customer) and Cedarline Tech (the Vendor) for the provision of managed services.

## 2. Definitions
Capitalised terms have the meaning given in Schedule 1. Business Day means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday.

## 3. Services
The Vendor shall provide the services described in Schedule 2 with reasonable skill and care, and shall meet the service levels set out in Schedule 3.

## 4.1 Payment Terms
Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within 30 days of receipt. Invoices are issued monthly in arrears.

## 5. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret and shall not disclose it except to personnel who need to know it.

## 7. Liability
The total aggregate liability of each party under this Agreement is limited to the total fees paid in the twelve months preceding the claim.

## 8. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of its courts.

## 9. Notices
Notices must be given in writing to the addresses set out in Schedule 4 and are deemed received on the next Business Day after delivery.
